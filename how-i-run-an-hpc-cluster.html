    How the stages hold together

    The point of running it as one flow: a problem in one stage usually shows up as a symptom in another.

    • Sizing at submit time (1) is what makes fair-share and license gates (2) work — a job sized wrong wastes cores no scheduler can give back.
    • Storage (4) is where solver jobs live or die: checkpointing and staging decide whether a node failure costs minutes or a whole run.
    • Monitoring (5) only earns its keep when it feeds triage (6) — a pattern tracked over weeks is what turns a hunch into a vendor case.
    • Lifecycle (7) closes the loop: patching, hardening, and onboarding are how yesterday’s fix becomes tomorrow’s default.
